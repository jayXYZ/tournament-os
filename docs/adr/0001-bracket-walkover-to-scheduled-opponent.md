# Bracket walkovers go to the scheduled opponent

Our posture is MTR-normative (see `CONTEXT.md`), but on playoff withdrawals we
deliberately read against the current text. MTR 2.10 (Feb 2026 revision) says
"the highest ranked remaining player receives a bye for the next round" —
literally, a re-award of the free win by Swiss seeding. We follow the 2015
MTR wording instead and award the uncontested match (recorded as a 2–0 Bye) to
the withdrawn player's **scheduled opponent**: bracket structure is sacred to
players — nobody expects their semifinal opponent to change because a
different quarterfinalist dropped — and the current phrasing reads like an
editing artifact, not an intended reseed. Defeated players are never revived
into the bracket, walkovers may chain (a final can be won by walkover), and
the departed player keeps the placement of the seat they reached.

Decided 2026-08-09. Textual analysis of both MTR revisions:
`docs/research/mtr-pairing-bye-dq-walkover.md`.
